Alpha Vision Business Holdings is a registered, diversified business enterprise established in Zimbabwe in 2019, with a long-term ambition to become a leading Pan-African investment holding company.

Our positioning

A diversified, investment-oriented business group with an operating portfolio and a long-term Pan-African investment ambition. We are building a platform to identify commercial opportunities, develop sustainable enterprises, establish strategic partnerships and create long-term economic value.

Long-term ambition. To establish Alpha Vision as a respected Pan-African investment holding company with a diversified portfolio of businesses, strategic investments and commercial partnerships.

Business and investment portfolio

Six strategic divisions operating under the registered parent company.

A. Investment & Business Development

Strategic investments and business development, enterprise establishment, acquisition opportunities, joint ventures and partnerships, and investment portfolio development.

B. Construction & Infrastructure

Residential and commercial construction, renovations, alterations and extensions, plastering and Rhinolite, waterproofing and damp-proofing, general maintenance and infrastructure opportunities.

C. Property & Real Estate

Property acquisition and disposal, commercial and residential sales and rentals, property management and maintenance, real estate investment and development partnerships.

D. Security & Risk Management

Security guarding, CCTV and electronic security, access control, biometric systems, security monitoring and risk management support.

E. Facilities Management & Cleaning

Commercial, residential and industrial cleaning, office cleaning, carpet, couch, upholstery and mattress cleaning, domestic cleaning and property maintenance support.

F. Technical Solutions & Events

CCTV and access control installations, turnstiles, boom gates, card readers, biometric and clocking systems, Wi-Fi and fibre, plus corporate events, conferences, product launches and logistics.

Corporate structure

Alpha Vision currently operates as one registered company with multiple business divisions and no separately incorporated subsidiaries. The long-term objective is a holding company model that can incorporate separate subsidiaries as activities expand, potentially in construction and infrastructure, property investment and development, security and technology, facilities management and strategic investment. Any future subsidiaries will be established through appropriate legal and regulatory processes.

Operating model. Registered parent company → six business divisions → future subsidiaries or investment entities as appropriate.
